Privacy Policy for Polytope Demo

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Zero-PII & Privacy by Design

The Polytope Demo (“the App”) is an interactive educational application designed to render and visualize regular polytopes and their cross-sectional slices in 2, 3, 4, and 5 dimensions. We adhere to strict Privacy by Design: we do not collect personal information, we do not require account registration, we do not track you across websites, and we do not sell or monetize user data.

This application is built for students, teachers, researchers, and geometry enthusiasts of all ages.

Information We Do NOT Collect

To maintain a zero-PII (Personally Identifiable Information) architecture:

  • No User Accounts: You can explore all polytopes and multidimensional projections without registering an account.

  • No Device Sensors or Invasive Permissions: The App does not access device location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts, or local file storage.

  • No Advertising or Cross-Site Tracking: The App is completely ad-free and contains no advertising networks, marketing trackers, or commercial analytics SDKs.

Data Handled by the Application

Local Settings & Preferences

  • Settings such as your preferred theme mode (light, dark, or system), custom theme colors, edge thickness, vertex dot radius, line clipping preferences, clipping gap size, rotation rates, and slice plane positions are stored exclusively on your local device using browser localStorage (on Web) or SharedPreferences (on Android).

  • These settings persist locally across sessions for user convenience.

  • On Web, clearing your browser application data or cache deletes these preferences. On Android, uninstalling the app removes all stored preferences.

  • No personal data or geometry configurations are ever transmitted to or stored on gchouse.org servers.

Third-Party Infrastructure

The App is hosted statically using Google Firebase Hosting (Google LLC):

  • Standard technical connection metadata (such as IP addresses and HTTP request headers) is processed temporarily by Google Cloud infrastructure strictly for routing, security, and denial-of-service prevention.

  • For more information on Google’s privacy practices, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Children & Classroom Safe (COPPA & FERPA)

The App is designed to be fully compliant with educational privacy regulations:

  • COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act): The App does not collect personal information from children under 13.

  • FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act): Because no student education records or personal identifiers are collected, maintained, or shared, the App can be safely used in K-12 and university classroom environments.
