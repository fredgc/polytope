Research: Regular Polytopes in ℝ3\mathbb{R}^3 and ℝ4\mathbb{R}^4

Summary of Answers

  • ℝ3\mathbb{R}^3 Polytopes (Platonic Solids): There are exactly 5 regular convex polyhedra in ℝ3\mathbb{R}^3. Polytope supports all 5.

  • ℝ4\mathbb{R}^4 Polytopes (Regular 4-Polytopes / Polychora): There are exactly 6 regular convex 4-polytopes in ℝ4\mathbb{R}^4.

  • Current ℝ4\mathbb{R}^4 Support in Codebase: Currently, 4 of the 6 are implemented:

    1. 5-cell (Regular Simplex, dim 4)
    2. 8-cell / Tesseract (Hypercube, dim 4)
    3. 16-cell / Orthoplex (Cross Polytope, dim 4)
    4. 24-cell (Octaplex / Icositetrachoron)

    The remaining 2 are:

    1. 120-cell (Hecatonicosachoron)
    2. 600-cell (Hexacosichoron)

3. Mathematical Foundations & Algebraic References

Binary Icosahedral Group and Quaternions

The 120 vertices of the 600-cell can be represented as unit quaternions forming the binary icosahedral group 2I2I. In 4D Euclidean space ℝ4\mathbb{R}^4, a quaternion q=x0+x1i+x2j+x3kq = x_0 + x_1 i + x_2 j + x_3 k sits on the 3-sphere S3⊂ℝ4S^3 \subset \mathbb{R}^4.

Vertex Coordinates

Let ϕ=1+52\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2} denote the golden ratio.

The 120 vertices of the unit 600-cell are: * 16 vertices: (±12,±12,±12,±12)(\pm \frac{1}{2}, \pm \frac{1}{2}, \pm \frac{1}{2}, \pm \frac{1}{2}) * 8 vertices: permutations of (±1,0,0,0)(\pm 1, 0, 0, 0) * 96 vertices: even permutations of (±ϕ2,±12,±12ϕ,0)(\pm \frac{\phi}{2}, \pm \frac{1}{2}, \pm \frac{1}{2\phi}, 0)

Edge and Face Adjacency

  • 600-cell: Two vertices are connected by an edge if and only if their Euclidean distance is 1/ϕ=ϕ−1≈0.6180341/\phi = \phi - 1 \approx 0.618034. Each vertex has degree 12, forming 720 edges. Each triangular face corresponds to a 3-clique of mutually connected vertices (1,200 faces). Each tetrahedral cell corresponds to a 4-clique (600 cells).

  • 120-cell (Dual Construction):

    • The 600 vertices are the circumcenters (averages of 4 vertices) of the 600 tetrahedral cells of the 600-cell, projected to the unit sphere S3S^3.
    • Two 120-cell vertices share an edge if their corresponding tetrahedra share a triangular face.
    • 120-cell faces are planar regular pentagons formed by cycles of 5 edges.
    • 120-cell cells are regular dodecahedra corresponding to the 120 vertices of the 600-cell.

4. Implementation Plan & Tool Architecture

To ensure high performance and avoid freeze or jank when opening these shapes:

  1. Pre-generation Tool (tool/generate_polychora.dart):
    • Computes the complete topological incidence mesh:
      • Vertices (x,y,z,w)(x, y, z, w)
      • Edges: pairs of vertex indices [v1,v2][v_1, v_2]
      • Faces: ordered sequences of edge indices forming closed loops
      • Cells: sets of face indices forming 3D boundary cells
    • Validates topological invariants (Euler characteristic V−E+F−C=0V - E + F - C = 0, vertex degrees, cell counts).
    • Serializes the meshes into compact JSON asset files:
      • assets/mesh/cell600.json
      • assets/mesh/cell120.json
  2. Asset Loading & Runtime Integration (Polytope.loadFromAsset):
    • Extend Polytope with a direct builder that reconstructs the incidence graph from precomputed index tables in O(V+E+F+C)O(V + E + F + C) time, avoiding slow combinatorial deduplication (gather()).
    • Add Regular.cell120 and Regular.cell600 to the Regular enum.
    • Connect with scene.dart and slice.dart to support full 4D rotation and real-time 3D hyperplane slicing.
