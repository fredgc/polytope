# Slices

To help visualize an n-dimensional polytope, this program displays the
intersection of the polytope with an (n-1)-dimensional hyperplane. The
intersection is also a convex polytope, but it might not be regular. When the
slice is active, a slider at the bottom of the screen controls the height or
position of the hyperplane.

To understand how it works, start with a 3D cube. Click the slice button.
Moving the slider shows that the intersection of a cube with a plane parallel
to a face is a square.

Rotating the cube to "Edge first" produces a rectangle that expands to a
square and shrinks back to an edge. Rotating to "Vertex first" produces a
triangle that morphs into a regular hexagon at the cube center.

Once familiar with 3D slices, try intersecting a 4D hypercube with a
3-dimensional hyperplane.
