# Controls

The top bar contains several controls:

1.  **Settings** (gear icon): Configure colors, edge/vertex sizes, line
    clipping, theme brightness, and on-screen controls.

2.  **Polytope Type**: Select the regular polytope to display (Simplex, Cube,
    Cross, Dodecahedron, Icosahedron, 24-cell, 120-cell, or 600-cell).

3.  **Dimension**: Choose the dimension (2 to 5).

4.  **Slice Toggle**: Turn the hyperplane cross-section slice on or off.

5.  **Movement / Transform Menu**: Choose the drag and button action
    (`Rotate R3`, `Rotate R4`, `Rotate R5`, `Zolly`, `Translate`, or `Scale`)
    or snap the orientation (`Vertex first`, `Edge first`, `Face first`, or
    `3-Cell first`).

6.  **Help** (`?`) and **Language** (globe icon): Open documentation or switch
    the interface and help language.

## Web & Desktop (Mouse & Keyboard)

-   **Drag**: Rotates, zooms/dollies (`Zolly`), translates, or scales according
    to the current movement mode.

-   **Scroll Wheel**: Zooms in or out.

-   **Fling**: Imparts velocity in `Rotate R3` or on the slice slider so motion
    continues smoothly.

-   **Click / Tap**: Stops spin on the canvas, or jumps and stops the slice
    slider.

-   **Arrow Keys**: Apply the current movement tool.

-   **`]` / `[`** (or **Page Up / Page Down**, **Numpad `+` / `-`**): Zoom in
    or out.

-   **Modifier Keys**: Hold **Shift** (`Rotate R4`), **Shift+Ctrl**
    (`Rotate R5`), **Ctrl** (`Zolly`), **Alt** (`Translate`), or **Ctrl+Alt**
    (`Scale`).

-   **`0`, `1`, `2`, `3`**: Snap to Vertex, Edge, Face, or 3-Cell first.

-   **Space**: Pauses rotation and slice animation when the canvas is focused.

-   **`+` / `-`** (or `=`): Speed up or slow down slice animation.

## Mobile (Touch)

-   **Drag, Fling & Tap**: Rotate, pan, impart spin, or tap to stop rotation.

-   **Pinch & Twist**: Two-finger pinch scales the polytope; twisting rotates
    in the screen plane.

-   **On-Screen Hotkey Buttons (`S`, `C`, `A`)**: Shown by default on phones
    and tablets (configurable in Settings) as touch equivalents for Shift,
    Ctrl, and Alt.

-   **On-Screen Pan/Zoom Buttons**: Optional in Settings. Tap an arrow to step,
    double-tap an arrow in `Rotate R3` to add spin, and tap the center Stop
    button to halt spin.

## Android TV (D-Pad Remote)

*This section applies to Android TV builds.*

-   **Navigation**: Use the D-pad arrows and **Select** to navigate the top
    bar, on-screen rotation/zoom buttons, and slice slider. Pressing **Down**
    from any top-bar control or **Up** from the slice slider moves focus to the
    center rotation button.

-   **Active Rotation Mode**:

    -   **Long-press Select** on the center **Stop** button to enter **Active
        Rotation Mode** (the center button changes to a highlighted globe
        icon).

    -   **Tap an arrow**: Increases rotation speed in that direction.

    -   **Long-press (hold) an arrow**: Steadily rotates as long as the button
        is held down.

    -   **Tap Select**: Stops rotation while staying in Active Rotation Mode.

    -   **Long-press Select** or press **Back**: Exits Active Rotation Mode.

-   **Slice Slider**: Focus the slice slider and press **Select** to start or
    stop forward animation, or use **Left / Right** to step manually.

-   **Settings**: Use **Up / Down** to step through settings one row at a time.
    On slider settings, press **Select** to enter edit mode, **Left / Right**
    to adjust the value, and **Select** or **Back** to finish.
