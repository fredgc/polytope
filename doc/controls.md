# Controls

The top bar contains several controls:

1. **Polytope Type**: Select the regular polytope to display.

2. **Dimension**: Choose the dimension (2 to 5).

3. **Slice Toggle**: Turn the hyperplane slice intersection on or off.

4. **Transform Menu**: Select rotation modes (R3, R4, R5), Zolly, or
   Translate.

## Gestures & Velocity

* **Drag**: Rotates or translates the polytope according to current mode.

* **Scroll / Pinch**: Zoom or scale the polytope.

* **Fling**: Imparts velocity so rotation or slice continues smoothly.

* **Tap**: Stops movement.

## Snaps

Quickly align the polytope so a vertex, edge, face, or 3-cell points directly
toward the screen.
