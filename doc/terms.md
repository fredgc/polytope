## 2. Educational & Geometric Demonstration Purpose Only

Polytope is an interactive mathematics application designed to project, rotate,
and slice higher-dimensional geometric objects (polytopes in 2, 3, 4, and 5
dimensions):

* The App is intended solely for personal, academic, classroom, and
  mathematical visualization purposes.

* User preferences (such as color themes, stroke widths, vertex sizes, and line
  clipping toggles) are retained locally on your client device for your
  convenience.

* It is not certified for CAD/CAM industrial fabrication, structural
  architecture, physics engineering, or precision machining.

---

## 3. Visual & Motion Sensitivity Notice

The App performs real-time continuous rotations in multi-dimensional space,
causing rapid morphing of projected wireframes and solid cross-sections:

* **Motion Sickness / Disorientation:** Viewing fast 4D/5D rotational
  projections may induce motion sensitivity, eye strain, or dizziness in some
  individuals.

* If you experience discomfort, please pause auto-rotation or reduce rotation
  speeds using the controls.
